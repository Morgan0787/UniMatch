# Decorative globe geography

`land-110m.json` is Natural Earth 1:110m land, downloaded 2026-10-08 from the
[Natural Earth vector repository](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson).

Natural Earth data is [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
It is used only to draw the decorative Home globe, not as university or admissions data.
The app builds its texture locally; no third-party requests are made at runtime.
